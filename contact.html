A few quick answers to the questions we hear most. Still unsure? Just message us.

How quickly will you reply?

We usually reply within one working day. For urgent questions, call or message us on WhatsApp during working hours.

Do I need a full plan before contacting you?

No. A rough idea is enough. We will help you shape the scope, features and priorities during the first call.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope and complexity. After understanding your requirements, we send a clear proposal with timeline and cost before any work starts.

Can we meet in person?

Yes, if you are in or near Ballari. Message us first to fix a time. Clients elsewhere can meet us online through video calls and screen sharing.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. We treat your project details and business data as confidential, and we are happy to sign an NDA before you share sensitive information.
