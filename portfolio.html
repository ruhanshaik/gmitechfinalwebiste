Common
Questions

Can't find your answer here? Call us or send a message and we will reply quickly.

How long does a typical project take?

A business website usually takes a few weeks. Larger systems such as HRMS, college management or CRM are built in stages, and we agree the timeline after understanding your requirements.

Can you customise a system for my business?

Yes. Every project is built around your workflow, so modules, reports, roles and branding can all be adapted to how your team actually works.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer training for your staff, bug fixes, updates and maintenance plans so your system stays reliable after go-live.

Can you add AI features to an existing system?

In most cases, yes. We can add chatbots, document search, automated reminders or reporting on top of your current software or database.

Do you work with businesses outside Ballari?

Yes. We are based in Ballari, Karnataka, but we work with clients across India through calls, screen sharing and regular online reviews.
